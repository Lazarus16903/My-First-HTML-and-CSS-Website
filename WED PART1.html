PolokwaneConnect

Table of Contents Website Goals and Objectives 1 Goals 1 Objectives 1 Proposed features and functionality 1 Features and Functionality 2 Design and User Experience 2 Conclusion 3 Name:PolokwaneConnect PolokwaneConnect is a web platform for local vendors and services. Most vendors, barbers, tutors and small businesses in Polokwane don’t have an easy way to reach customers online. People rely on applications like Facebook and WhatsApp to advertise their things. As a result, many miss customers and people miss out on good local services. Website Goals and Objectives The main purpose of this website is so that people in Polokwane with local vendors,services and products Goals • Connect the people around Polokwane residents can find local vendors and services • Supporting local businesses helping them get more visibility and customers • Make access easy with low-data mobile friendly platform that anyone can use Objectives • Allow vendors to register and create profiles with products, prices and contact info • Enable users to search, filter and browse vendors by category and location • Add a chat and order feature so customers can contact vendors directly • Ensure the website and app work well on your laptops,computers and your phone as well, with limited data • Launch a test version with 10 vendors in Polokwane Proposed features and functionality A responsive website + mobile app that serves as directory and ordering platforms for vendors,service providers and small business in Polokwane. Think Facebook Marketplace but only for Polokwane, but built simpler and low-data Features and Functionality 1.User Features • Sign up/Login: Phone number or email. Students, residents and vendors van register • Search and Filters:Seach by category, location, price • Vendor profiles to be able to see photos, bio, products, prices, location pin, contact details • Chat system: direct message vendors without sharing numbers • Order/booking in order to request a service or product and track status • Reviews & Ratings: Rate vendors after using the service 2.Vendor Features • Business Dashboard:Add/Edit products, update prices, upload pictures • Notifications:Get alerts when someone messages or orders • Analytics: See how many people viewed their profile • Low-data mode:Compresss images so it works on cheap data 3.Admin Features • Verify vendors:Approve new businesses to avoid scams • Manage categories: Food,clothes,tutoring, hair, transport etc • Report system: Users can report fake profiles 4.Tecnical Functionality • Responsive design: Works on phone, tablet and laptop • Map integration: Show vendor location in Polokwane • Fast loading: Optimized for slow network Design and User Experience Design Clean community vibe colors inspired by Polokwane The style will represent Polokwane User Experience [UX] 1.First Time User:Land on Home>See”Popular Vendors Near You”>Search bar at top>2 clicks to find what you need 2.Vendor onboarding:3 steps only.Sign up>Add business name+photo>post first product done 3.Low-data priority:Images load in low quality first. ‘Tap load HD’ button.No auto-play videos 4.Mobile-first:Big buttons, thumb-friendly. Works on cheap smartphones. 5.Trust:Verified badge on real vendors.Reviews visible. Chats keeps both numbers private Conclusion PolokwaneConnect will solve a real life problem. If we build it with users in mind it can actually help people make money.
